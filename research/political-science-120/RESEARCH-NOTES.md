## Initial content implications

Use the ten UGC subject units as the organizing spine: theory; political thought; Indian thought; comparative analysis; international relations; Indian foreign policy; Indian institutions; Indian political processes; public administration; governance/public policy.

The RPSC comparison requires additions beyond the national outline, including some thinkers, named national constitutional systems and Rajasthan politics. Kerala requires research/teaching and local social-political history. HPPSC requires Himachal politics and additional contemporary world-order coverage. These additions should be tagged to the actual recruitment cycle; do not describe common subject coverage as complete preparation for every state exam.

RPSC's sampled questions include four substantive answer choices and a fifth administrative unattempted choice. The current website accepts four choices, so retain substantive options and use the website's skip control for practice. Do not claim identical OMR or scoring behaviour. Existing question marks default to 2; exam-specific marking, penalties and timing must be verified before offering exam simulations.

HPPSC 2022 revised answers include deleted questions and questions with more than one accepted answer. The website currently supports one correct index. Exclude such items from scored practice unless reviewed and explicitly adapted; maintain the original key status in the research record.

## Provisional allocation (not finalized)

120 tests can be organized as 100 topic tests (10 per national unit) plus 20 revision/mixed tests. Final distribution should follow complete syllabus mapping and the supplied PYQ analysis, rather than assuming equal unit importance. State extensions may require reallocating slots or a separate supplement. A comprehensive all-state promise cannot be made from this initial review.
